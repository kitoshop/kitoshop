THE KITO SPIRIT

An expression
of individuality.

25 years of fashion & craftsmanship in Saigon

KITO is a boutique for those who appreciate character over convention. From expressive fashion to collectible ceramics, every discovery celebrates colour, heritage and the pleasure of finding something unique.

For 25 years, KITO has welcomed visitors from around the world, many of whom return to discover something new. Fashion and traditional crafts, selected with an individual point of view.
